# Judge Demo Flow

1. Start Nexora on port 8200.
2. Present it as a normal enterprise company, not a cybersecurity dashboard.
3. Browse Home → Solutions → Customer Portal.
4. Show that the site is backed by APIs and data, not just static HTML.
5. Show `/admin`, `/api/profile`, `/api/search`, and `/uploads` as examples of security-assessment surfaces.
6. Explain that the wider Nexora enterprise estate contains the exact 30 shortlisted CVEs.
7. In the security-tool phase, scan the isolated environment with the team's OpenVAS/Wazuh/ZAP setup.
8. Export findings.
9. Feed those findings into the separate CyberFinGuard project.
